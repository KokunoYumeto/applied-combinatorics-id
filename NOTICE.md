# Attribution, changes, and non-endorsement

This repository publishes **Kombinatorika Terapan — Edisi Bahasa Indonesia**, a maintained Indonesian translation/adaptation of *Applied Combinatorics*, Edition 2017.3, by Mitchel T. Keller and William T. Trotter.

The official source was frozen at commit `33b20df670d1f8d98266cd2f4a287a79b01649ea`. Changes include translation to `id-ID`, localized reader/accessibility surfaces, explicitly recorded source corrections, deterministic packaging, and a modular backend.
